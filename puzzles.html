The 1924 Crossword

In 1924 America went crossword-crazy, and the very first crossword books flew off the shelves. Every day we set out one of their puzzles, clues and all, exactly as readers saw them a hundred years ago. Some of the words are old-fashioned, so think like your great-grandmother.
