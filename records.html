    Why the radio stops at 1925

    In the U.S., records made in 1925 or earlier are in the public domain, so The Bee's Knees can play them on the radio for free. They play on Gramophone Radio, channel 08.

    Anything newer needs a broadcasting license, and the station can't afford one yet. Until then, the swing, blues, crooners and rock 'n' roll live here on the shelf, and you play them straight from Archive.org, where they're kept.
