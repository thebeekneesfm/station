About this station

A radio dial of old shows and 78s, playing all the time. Every station was on before you got here.

How it works

You don't pick what plays. You tune in, and whatever is on is on, for you and for everyone else on that station at the same second. That's how radio worked.

It costs nothing. There's no account, no ads and nothing to buy.

Whose recordings these are

Not mine. Every show and record plays straight from the Internet Archive, a non-profit library. This site hosts no audio of its own. Your browser plays the Archive's file, and "via archive.org" under each title opens that recording's page at the Archive, where you can see who uploaded it.

Listening here uses the Archive's bandwidth, the same as listening there. They run on donations, so if you enjoyed something, give them something.

This station isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Internet Archive. Much of the radio collection was gathered and labeled by the Old Time Radio Researchers, and the 78s by the people who digitized them. The credit goes to them.

What's mine: the schedule, the drawings and the station breaks.

What's on

If something won't play

Recordings sometimes disappear from the Archive. When that happens the station plays static until the next show starts, so nobody falls out of step. I check for dead recordings and take them off the schedule.

If something shouldn't be here

If you uploaded a recording or hold the rights to one and want it off a station, get in touch with the station name and the title, and it will come off the schedule. Removing the file from the Archive itself is a separate request to them, and they explain how to make it.

The 78s on this station were all released in 1925 or earlier, which puts them in the US public domain. The radio shows were broadcast between the 1930s and early 1960s and are widely treated as public domain, but not every part of every show is settled.

What's kept

  • No account and no cookies.
  • Your browser remembers your last station and volume, on your own device.
  • The "listening" counts come from a random number your tab makes when you turn the radio on. It only says which station you're on, it goes away when you close the tab, and no one, including me, can tell who it belongs to. The count runs on Supabase, which sees the connection while it's open. Nothing about you is saved there.
  • Your browser fetches audio from archive.org. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, and like any host, GitHub sees your connection in its own server logs.

A note about the shows

These programs are played as they were made. Some are frightening, and some carry the attitudes of their time, including stereotypes that were wrong then too. Nothing is rated or filtered.
