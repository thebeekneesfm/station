How the drive-in works

It runs on a clock, like a real drive-in. Everyone watching sees the same moment, so you join the show wherever it’s at.

Every night has a theme and a new double feature, listed below. The full show starts at the same moment for everyone: 7:30 PM Pacific, which is where you are. It runs like tonight’s program here.

Before the show there’s a matinee, and after the show there’s a late show until midnight. Both play more films from that night’s theme back to back, so there’s always something on the screen.
