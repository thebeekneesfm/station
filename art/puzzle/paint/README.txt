Paintings for the Community Puzzle (puzzle-together.html), one per entry in the Supabase table puzzle_lineup,
named by the Art Institute of Chicago image id. Public domain (CC0) images from the Art Institute of Chicago,
saved here 1600 px wide so the puzzle doesn't depend on the museum's image server. "-small" is the stream thumbnail.
To add a painting: add a row to puzzle_lineup and save its image here the same way.
